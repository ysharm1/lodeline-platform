## The rule

DFARS 252.225-7052, *Restriction on the Acquisition of Certain Magnets, Tantalum, and Tungsten*. The clause version shown on eCFR and Acquisition.gov when this plan was written is dated **May 2024** (final rule DFARS Case 2021-D015, published May 30, 2024, implementing section 854 of the FY2024 NDAA, Pub. L. 118-31, and 10 U.S.C. 4872).

- **Through December 31, 2026:** no covered material *melted or produced* in a covered country, and no end item *manufactured* in a covered country that contains covered material.
- **From January 1, 2027:** the restriction adds *mining, refining and separation*. For magnets it covers the whole chain from mining through finished magnets; for tantalum and tungsten it covers ore or feedstock (including recycled material) through metal, powder, alloy or component.
- **Covered countries:** China, Russia, Iran, North Korea.
- **Covered materials:** SmCo magnets, NdFeB magnets, tantalum metals and alloys, tungsten metal powder, tungsten heavy alloy and components containing it.
- The clause does not apply at or below the simplified acquisition threshold and has exceptions (see paragraph (c)).

In our product language: from January 1, 2027, no mining, separation/refining, melting/alloy or magnet-making step may happen in a covered country.

**Recommendation:** get a legal check of the current clause version, exceptions and flow-down wording before we put clause text on the website, in packs or in sales emails. Re-check the clause date each time we publish.

## What is free

1. **Parts-list check.** A customer uploads a parts list (no drawings). We say which parts likely contain rare earth magnets, tungsten or tantalum, and which look high-risk. Output is a likely / possible / not likely list with reasons.
2. **China-free supplier directory / marketplace.** Searchable list of suppliers outside covered countries, by stage, material, region and country. Listings start as unverified prospects from public sources (see 04).

The free tools exist to start conversations that lead to a paid trace.

## How a trace works

1. Customer sends a parts list and supplier names. No controlled drawings, no CUI, no ITAR/EAR technical data.
2. We send supplier questionnaires up the chain, tier by tier, until we reach the mine. Dylan and Delilah cover Taiwan and wider Asia in Mandarin.
3. Suppliers return certificates and declarations. AI reads them and proposes records; **a human confirms every record**.
4. We flag any step that is in China, Russia, Iran or North Korea, and any step with no documents.
5. We deliver:
   - a per-part evidence pack PDF, with each claim cited to a page of a source document;
   - an exceptions list (covered-country steps and undocumented steps);
   - China-free replacement suppliers for each open stage, from the marketplace.

Lodeline records what the documents say. It does not issue a compliance finding (see 05).
